# Additional source-constrained sensitivity

This extension was specified after seeing the original pass 4 sensitivity results. It is explicitly post hoc and must not replace the wider envelope.

The working severe-normal-tissue-injury criterion in the earlier coding guide allows human grade 3+ injury with direct or author-probable on-target attribution. Under that criterion, the reviewed CD47 anemia and NECTIN4 skin records have support for an unfavorable observation in their stated contexts. Independently adjudicated gene-level labels are still unavailable.

Therefore also report the subset of the already evaluated 1,024 assignments in which CD47 and NECTIN4 are unfavorable. This leaves 256 assignments over eight uncertain genes. No scores change, no new assignment is invented, and both the full and constrained ranges must be shown. The other 19 labels remain inherited assumptions, including all 16 fixed favorable labels. This is a conditional sensitivity exercise, not a clinically valid binary dataset.
