# Outcome contract v0.2: observational evidence, not intrinsic safety

October 2, 2026. Advisory analysis; developed after seeing historical outcomes and sensitivity results. Not preregistered or independently adjudicated. Extends pass 2's coding guide without changing historical labels.

## Common question

In a named human agent/cohort, was clinically significant injury to normal tissue attributed to recognition of the intended target? Record grade >=3, injury-related cessation, and death separately. Direct support and author-probable attribution qualify for the working event category; author-possible and unresolved attribution remain uncertain. A protocol DLT is a separate field, not the severity definition. Approval, efficacy, manageable symptoms, and program termination cannot determine this endpoint.

The unit is agent/cohort/event and observation window. A gene is a grouping variable, not a universal safety verdict. Different agents, routes, isoforms, combinations and cohorts remain distinguishable. Multiple papers reporting overlapping patients cannot become independent observations.

## Three observational states, plus eligibility

- **event_supported:** qualifying severity and target attribution are documented in a human treatment context. Record the evidence supporting each criterion.
- **explicit_nonobservation:** the source explicitly reports no specified event or its table gives zero for that named event and grade. This is a scoped observation, never an automatic negative label for all normal-tissue injury.
- **indeterminate:** silence, broad tolerability language, unresolved mechanism, missing severity, or an endpoint mismatch. Do not convert this state to favorable or unfavorable.
- **ineligible:** preclinical-only evidence for a human endpoint or unresolvable agent/antigen identity. Retain separately with the reason.

These states apply per event. One study can have a supported event and a nonobservation for a different event. A record of severe drug-associated toxicity with unresolved target mechanism stays indeterminate for this particular endpoint, while retaining the observed severe toxicity on its own axis.

## Normal-cell depletion and its consequences

Apply the same rules to blood and nonblood tissues. Extract depletion, loss of function, clinical consequences, supportive care, recovery and cessation in separate fields. Do not exempt injury because the depleted lineage is renewable or replacement care exists. Conversely, depletion alone does not prove a grade >=3 injury or establish the cause of every later infection.

Supportive care is part of the observed treatment context. A low complication rate under replacement care cannot establish low untreated risk. Separate target-associated depletion from conditioning, prior treatments, disease, payload and immune activation when the evidence permits. Otherwise retain causal uncertainty.

## Observation adequacy: mandatory fields, no invented cutoff

For an explicit nonobservation extract: treated and endpoint-evaluable denominators; named endpoint and grading; exposure/activity evidence; ascertainment method; observation window and censoring; missingness; relevant supportive care; treatment context; and overlap with other cohorts.

Report each as known, missing, or not applicable, with a source location. Median survival follow-up is not automatically toxicity follow-up. Enrollment is not automatically the safety denominator. Activity in some patients does not establish adequate exposure in every patient.

No minimum cohort size, minimum follow-up or acceptable risk threshold is selected in this pass. A zero-event bound would describe only a specified outcome under explicit sampling/detection assumptions, not validate a target-level favorable class. A binary amended panel is not ready until the adequacy criteria and causal judgments are independently reviewed. Continue collecting observational states rather than forcing a binary answer.

## Evaluation gate

Before any new performance claim: reconcile agent/cohort/event records, freeze endpoint and eligibility decisions with provenance, obtain score-withheld initial review, and document disagreements. Preserve the current fixed score and the confinement/tau and blood-only comparisons. Group resampling by target if multiple studies per target are used; do not increase the effective sample size merely by adding overlapping papers. Do not train or choose an endpoint to maximize this panel's AUC.

The existing 19 favorable labels are not adjudicated negatives. Pass 4's ranges remain conditional on its fixed labels, including labels this pass questions. This pass produces no replacement AUC and no independently validated clinical endpoint.
