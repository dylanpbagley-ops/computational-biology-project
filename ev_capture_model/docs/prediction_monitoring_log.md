## Log maintenance rules

- Entries are appended. Prior entries are not modified or deleted.
- A correction to a prior entry is recorded as a new dated entry that identifies the entry
  corrected and the reason.
- Only a quarterly formal check resolves a prediction (§B2–B4). Interim monitoring entries
  have no resolution authority.
- Each entry lists its sources: trial registries, primary publications, and FDA/EMA labels
  and review documents. Preclinical-only evidence does not resolve a prediction.
- Ambiguous findings are recorded as unresolved.

## Monitoring schedule

Monthly checks, with additional targeted checks following ESMO proceedings (~November 2026)
and ASH (December 2026), and before each fair (school fair ~late January 2027; AzSEF senior
judging March 26, 2027).
