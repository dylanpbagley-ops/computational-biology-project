# Angle 4 candidate dossier -- PRE-COMMITMENT

**Recorded 2026-09-27, BEFORE any investigation of the candidate.**

This file exists so the dossier cannot be accused of candidate-shopping. The selection rule
and the candidate are fixed here first; whatever the investigation finds is reported.

## Selection rule (fixed before looking)

From `screen_rerank_weighted.json` (continuous weights, gated at top_share >= 0.25), take the
highest-ranked candidate that is:
  1. not already in clinical development (the screen's own `NEW` filter), AND
  2. not an immunoglobulin variable-region gene (IGKV*) -- those score highly in myeloma
     largely as an artifact of plasma-cell clonality, not because they are novel targets.

## Candidate selected

Rank 2 and 3 are ERVFRD-1 and PSG1 (syncytiotrophoblast/placental). Rank 4 is DCT.

**Selected: DCT** (dopachrome tautomerase), confinement 0.948, one cell type (melanocytes),
361x elevated in skin cancer cell lines.

Chosen over the two placental antigens because melanocyte loss has a documented clinical
phenotype (vitiligo) that is known to be tolerated, which gives the expendability claim an
empirical anchor rather than a developmental-absence argument.

## Committed in advance

- The investigation may find DCT is unsuitable. If so that is the reported result.
- Specific things that would kill it: already an active antibody/CAR target (not novel);
  not accessible on the cell surface; no evidence tumours depend on it.
- No substitute candidate will be selected if DCT fails. A failure is a finding about the
  screen's limits and will be written up as one.
