# B pilot specification — frozen before numerical results

October 2, 2026. Exploratory reuse of an already inspected dataset. Historical work measured NBM expression; this pilot adds explicit donor-conditioned threshold generalization. Not a novel ROC method or clinical-response model.

Data: GSE193531 extracted_markers.json matched by exact cell ID order to original compressed metadata. NBM/normal is reference; MM/neoplastic is positive group. All 9 NBM and 8 MM samples included, no cell-count exclusions. MGUS/SMM excluded by disease definition. Three established extracted antigens: TNFRSF17, GPRC5D, SLAMF7. Source study identifies samples as distinct individuals. Clinical outcomes are absent. Age, sex, fresh/frozen and batch are unresolved possible confounders; a donor effect cannot automatically be called biological.

Measured scale: UMI/n_counts * 10,000; no imputation; zeros remain not-detected. Compare only distributions of the same gene. RNA abundance is not surface protein, binding or killing. Total-count normalization may be sensitive to immunoglobulin composition; disclose this rather than treating it as calibration. Raw UMI is a secondary assay sensitivity check, not an extra source of biological units.

Response class: deterministic upper thresholds f(x)=1[x>=t], including respond-all and respond-none. Enumerate all distinct measured values exactly; no binned/grid universal theorem. Normal positive fraction is an assay-defined fraction of measured cells, not clinical harm. Primary normal fraction budget b=0.10 chosen as an illustrative assay operating point, not safe-risk criterion. Fixed secondary b=0.05,0.20,0.50 show the curve, avoiding selection of a favorable point.

Compare three selection policies: pooled cells, equal-donor average, and donor-envelope (one threshold satisfying every observed NBM donor while maximizing the worst MM donor fraction). Pooled/equal-donor optimize their corresponding MM average under the corresponding normal average budget. Because upper thresholds are nested, choose smallest feasible threshold to maximize every MM tail; tie convention is smallest threshold. Empirical donor convex hulls have extremal expectations at donors, so max normal/min MM matches their envelopes. This covers observed donor mixtures only, with no guarantee for unseen donors or population distributions. No swap between per-donor oracle and shared response.

Primary feasibility estimand: worst observed normal exceedance and worst MM coverage of the average-selected threshold versus donor-envelope threshold. Baseline: equal-donor mean-expression ratio (descriptive, not a classifier) and pooled threshold. Positive finding must show an interpretation changes or a missing measurement identified, beyond known higher expression. Disconfirmation: negligible mismatch and no practical antigen-comparison consequence; no novelty claim if closest work already performs this evaluation.

Internal held-out check: all 8x9 leave-one-MM/one-NBM sample pairs; determine thresholds using only remaining donors and their measured values. Evaluate held-out MM coverage and normal budget exceedance. These 72 overlapping folds are dependent, not 72 biological subjects; report 8/9 donor counts and donor-level summaries. No fitting/tuning, and no honest claim of independent external validation. No p-value for fold counts. Donor bootstrap (seed 20261002, 2000 resamples) may describe equal-donor point-estimate uncertainty; not a confidence guarantee on extremes.

Acceptance tests: tie/direction, none/all thresholds, normal zero mass, small finite exact enumeration, donor rather than cell weights, disjoint held-out sample IDs, invalid counts/missingness rejection, protected input hashes. Full changed entry point must run. Gate after pilot: proceed only if scientifically interpretable, contribution beyond known expression and existing sensitivity is identifiable, and data support a convincing evaluation. Otherwise record no-go/conditional status.
