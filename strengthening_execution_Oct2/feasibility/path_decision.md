A substantial new biological or clinically predictive contribution is not demonstrated. Retain A as a conditional future route requiring independent endpoint review and much more primary cohort extraction; do not fit a context model now.

This is the required decision checkpoint, not a declaration that all feasibility gates passed. Do not expand this into two full projects or continue general literature/audit rounds.

## A: context-aware clinical evaluation

1. Claim beyond original: pre-outcome agent context improves unseen-target outcome evaluation, or quantified applicability limits generalize beyond illustrative cases. Neither is currently supported.
2. Closest work: SCAN-ACT normal-expression/target selection; BayesTS normal-only multimodal specificity; Madan et al. selectivity/safety scoring; Thomas et al. expression versus clinical toxicity. See prior_art.tsv. Accessible evidence differs from the proposed target-recognition endpoint. CAIX blocking already demonstrates context dependence in the primary trial, so repeating it is not the contribution.
3. Opened: full 166-row local source index; 35-row deterministic pilot covering eight multi-agent leads and four controls; prior extractions and fresh CD19/ALFA/CAIX batch. Fifteen pilot rows are regulatory summaries, not unique trial cohorts. CAIX initial/final reports duplicate patients; CD52 brands share one agent; CD44 CAR lacks a publication in this index. The pilot source full-text pass is incomplete and all included records retain their gaps.
4. Units: 12 workload targets, not 35 independent targets. Gene mapping, isoforms, overlapping patients, routes, diseases, payloads, supportive care and observation opportunity confound comparisons. One supported cessation/explicit liver nonobservation contrast is known, tiny and within one trial/target; no general binary outcome diversity is established.
5. Disconfirmation: consistent endpoint adjudication finds that context does not improve target-held-out evaluation, or apparent discordance disappears after monitoring/attribution matching.
6. Baseline/evaluation: unchanged rule versus small pre-outcome context model on target-held-out groups; requires endpoint review first. No model or binary metric now. Within-gene contrast cannot test between-gene ranking.
7. Deliverable by school fair: supporting clinical evidence component is feasible; a validated new context predictor is conditional on named independent clinical review and supportable negative observations. No sample-size sufficiency inferred from row counts.
8. Retain fixed score and source inventory. New work would be substantial adjudication/normalization plus precision planning. Initial extraction doesn't establish feasibility of the primary claim. **A is not selected as primary now; failure to meet the current gate is not proof the route is impossible.**

## B: a common assay threshold across measured donors

1. Specific new claim to test: an average-compatible RNA threshold for TNFRSF17, GPRC5D or SLAMF7 may exceed its normal-cell fraction budget in individual healthy donors; protecting every measured donor can change malignant-cell coverage and antigen comparison. Can observed-donor protection transfer to a withheld donor? This is a measurement question, not a clinical safety claim.
2. Closest work: Boiarsky source study already establishes plasma-cell transcriptomic heterogeneity; Smith et al. and Verkleij et al. already compare malignant/normal GPRC5D and document target heterogeneity. Classical threshold/ROC and extremal mixture geometry already supplies the math. Candidate contribution is the exact, reproducible common-threshold and held-out-donor audit on these measurements, not discovery of GPRC5D selectivity or a new theorem. Biological importance beyond a useful limitation remains uncertain.
3. Opened: 29,387-cell extracted count JSON and original cell metadata matched by cell ID. Eligible groups: 9 NBM donors (9,329 cells), 8 MM samples with source-labeled neoplastic cells. No new downloads or wet lab needed. Raw-count extraction has a mistaken historical DOI in its header; correct source association is GSE193531 / 10.1038/s41467-022-33944-z; preserve original file and record correction here.
4. Units: 17 individuals. Source labels are expression-derived, not independent functional identity assays. Cell depth, immunoglobulin composition, fresh/frozen, age/sex and batch may contribute to donor differences. No matched whole-body protein reference or causal clinical outcomes. One donor has only 51 cells; no post-hoc exclusion.
5. Disconfirmation: average thresholds show negligible individual mismatch, held-out findings do not persist, raw-count check reverses interpretation, or source uncertainty accounts for the apparent biological finding. A mismatch still demonstrates limits of the observed measurement, even when its biological cause remains unknown.
6. Baselines/evaluation: pooled cells, equal-donor mean and donor-envelope thresholds; mean-expression ratio as descriptive baseline; exact finite threshold enumeration. Internal all-pairs leave-one-NBM/one-MM-out evaluation with 72 dependent folds and 17 individuals, not independent validation. Donor resampling gives descriptive uncertainty. Future calibrated protein would answer a different measurement question; current ADT cohort cannot substitute for normal-plasma-cell RNA.
7. Smallest credible deliverable: completed within-dataset measurement audit, figures and reproducibility, with explicit conditional medical relevance. Computationally feasible now; completed numerical pilot at 10% budget already shows mismatch and loss of coverage. January timeline allows student source verification and explanation. A stronger novel biological inference would require named matched assay/batch metadata or an independent comparable normal/malignant RNA reference; neither is secured.
8. Retain distribution foundation and existing data; add donor-specific threshold policies/evaluation rather than repeat the old pooled NBM detection result or calibration search. **Proceed with the narrow analysis; classify the broader substantive extension as conditional, and project strength as modest until external/biological validation and novelty are established.**
