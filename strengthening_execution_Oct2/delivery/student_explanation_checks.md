# Student explanation checks

Explain each in your own words before writing. These prompts are not submission prose.

- What pairwise event does AUC count, and why is 0.914 not 91.4% clinical accuracy?
- Why do 35 source rows, 72 dependent folds and thousands of cells not produce 35/72/thousands of independent targets or donors?
- How can injury be severe without being a protocol DLT? Why does approval not mean no injury?
- Why can B-cell depletion support an intended-target effect while infection causality remains unresolved? What does immunoglobulin care change about a no-injury conclusion?
- Why does no liver toxicity in four patients not establish gene-level safety? What observation window and missingness would a reviewer need?
- What is withheld from the reviewer, and why is assistant triage not independent review?
- What measured quantity is thresholded in B? Why are RNA UMIs and antibody/protein molecules different?
- What is the difference between satisfying the pooled mean and satisfying every observed donor? Work through the 99 low cells / one high cell donor counterexample in the tests.
- What does the illustrative 10% budget mean, and why is it not acceptable clinical risk?
- Why does the observed-donor envelope fail for a withheld donor? Which uncertainty set was actually covered?
- Which response class was optimized? Why is exact enumeration over empirical thresholds not a universal population theorem or an arbitrary-response ceiling?
- How do raw-count sensitivity, preparation/batch and source-derived cell labels limit a biological explanation?
- What did the extension add beyond the original association? What had Smith/Verkleij/Boiarsky already established? Which novelty claim remains unsupported?
- What missing independent reference or matched assay would change the conclusion, and what would disconfirm it?
