# Contribution assessment — advisory, not submission prose

**Modestly stronger; substantial new biological/clinical contribution remains unestablished.** The selected narrow B analysis is completed. It demonstrates a specific limitation of the existing measurements, rather than only repairing historical labels. The clinical A extension is conditional and not selected for modeling. See ../feasibility/path_decision.md for the bounded decision.

Before this work: the project had a retrospective gene-level clinical association, valid finite distribution mathematics under explicit assumptions, and an NBM pooled BCMA expression check. Published work already established normal/malignant GPRC5D expression and antigen heterogeneity; the source dataset already studied plasma-cell transcriptomic variation. Normal-expression target selection and ROC geometry are established concepts.

Added: a common, deterministic RNA threshold evaluated across actual healthy and malignant plasma-cell donors, contrasted with pooled/equal-donor policies, and tested with donors withheld. No gene-only clinical association can answer this measurement question. This is an empirical extension using retained biological assets, not a new antigen, new ROC theorem or independently confirmed clinical predictor.

At the illustrative 10% measured-normal-cell budget, the pooled BCMA threshold covers 24.9% of pooled myeloma cells but reaches 24.6% in the most exposed healthy donor. The observed-donor envelope threshold reduces pooled myeloma coverage to 9.0% while meeting every measured healthy-donor budget. Equal-donor average weighting alone does not solve the individual-donor constraint: BCMA's worst normal fraction is 27.6%. Each number concerns RNA in CD138-selected plasma cells, not cell killing or clinical harm. See b_summary.tsv and b_observed_donor_tradeoff figure.

Internal withholding exposes the strongest limitation of the envelope itself. For BCMA, pooled policy exceeds 10% in three of nine held-out healthy donors; envelope policy in one of nine. That withheld donor reaches 17.2% even under the envelope policy. GPRC5D and SLAMF7 envelope policies also fail the withheld normal constraint in one donor each. The 72 leave-pair-out folds share individuals; they do not create 72 independent subjects. No p-value or independent-validation claim is made from them. See b_held_out.tsv and b_held_out_donors figure.

The best competing explanation is measurement/context dependence: library depth, immunoglobulin composition, fresh/frozen preparation, age, sex, batch and source-derived cell labels can contribute. Raw UMI analysis preserves the presence of donor-budget violations but changes coverage and some rankings. GPRC5D's observed-envelope pooled MM coverage is 10.2% on normalized RNA and only 2.5% on raw counts; raw counts are themselves depth-confounded. These are not two independent biological replications. Donor-bootstrap intervals are broad (BCMA equal-donor mean MM coverage 6.4%–36.3%, conditional on this empirical sampling/model), and do not prove antigen superiority or population bounds.

Closest prior work narrows the contribution. Smith et al. already documents variable GPRC5D/BCMA protein expression; Verkleij et al. already evaluates malignant/normal target expression. Boiarsky et al. already describes heterogeneity and uses the same data. The eight-paper prior_art.tsv comparison found no basis for claiming a novel mathematical principle or that nobody tested related limitations. The defensible added contribution is this explicit, reproducible donor-transportability assessment on the named assay/groups. Whether that is sufficiently consequential for the project's desired impact remains open.

The clinical repair now includes a versioned common endpoint, 44-record screening frame, six split event observations from four study/cohort units, overlap records and neutral review materials. The pooled CD19 primary report documents late severe infections under replacement care; attribution to intended-target depletion remains unresolved. The source also contains an internally inconsistent hypogammaglobulinemia denominator, retained as a gap rather than silently corrected. Original AUC is reproduced at 0.913875598086, conditional on unchanged 19/11 labels. No amended labels or safety verdicts are manufactured.

Unestablished: independent clinical review; adequate all-organ negative observations; a general context model; source-selection bias correction; clinical calibration of RNA response; protein confirmation of this result; functional malignant/normal discrimination; independent comparable-dataset replication; and substantial scientific novelty. The inspected local ADT assay has different measurement/comparator/gating and cannot supply that replication. Future data/metadata are named dependencies, not promised resources.

Next stopping decision: no general new candidate search or more clinical resampling. A stronger B claim requires accessible matched assay/batch information and/or a comparable independent plasma-cell reference. A stronger A claim requires the prepared independent source/endpoint review plus supportable clinical comparisons.
