# Bounded fallback screens

## F1 — depth-limited transcript detection

**Actual pilot:** local GSE193531 integer UMI matrix; 9 NBM donors (9,329 eligible cells) and 8 MM donors (10,759 neoplastic cells). All matrix columns were reconciled to `n_counts`; fixed common depth was 5,189 UMIs/cell; 100 hypergeometric draws; seed `20261003`; the same retained cells were compared before/after thinning. Thirty-one MM-stage cells labeled Normal were excluded. Median TNFRSF17 donor detection fell from 0.9025 to 0.7039 in NBM and from 0.9303 to 0.7331 in MM after thinning; these are descriptive donor summaries, and the Monte Carlo intervals quantify thinning draws, not patient sampling. Retention ranged down to 65.0% in NBM and 20.7%/26.2% in MM-1/MM-8, so the thinned subset is not interchangeable with the full cohort.

**Closest comparison and decision:** the existing GSE193531 analyses and GSE305971 target-ranking study compare malignant with `Normal_PC` expression, but neither is treated as direct prior art for this depth-identifiability pilot. This screen does not claim that those studies exhaust the question. It demonstrates that finite depth changes observed transcript detection on the same labeled cells; it cannot determine whether a low count reflects true absence, measure protein, or provide a validated measurement-error model linking RNA to antigen availability. Thus it does not answer the fallback's intended antigen-selectivity-identifiability question. **Fails as primary extension; retain as a depth-sensitivity result, with novelty of that measurement question unresolved.** Sources: `analysis/results/metadata_diagnostics.json`, `metadata_thinning_paired.tsv`, and `metadata_associations.tsv`.

## F2 — uncertainty-aware clinical evidence benchmark

**Actual applicability screen:** 34 normalized event/grade rows across two target/source families and two fixed clinical studies: TNFRSF17/ide-cel KarMMa and GPRC5D/talquetamab MonumenTAL-1. All 34 rows have `observation_state=indeterminate`; zero are independently adjudicated event-supported target endpoints, and zero target families have an independent clinical reviewer. Evidence includes ide-cel hypogammaglobulinemia 27/128 any grade and 1/128 grade 3/4; infection 88/128 and 28/128. MonumenTAL-1 Table 2 has six event families in RP2D cohorts (n=30 weekly 405 and n=44 every-other-week 800), including grade-specific counts; grade 3/4 dysgeusia remains source NA. Three grade 3 rash dose-limiting cases and a separate lipase DLT are documented with NR dose-escalation denominators.

**Closest comparison and decision:** this is a source-description and applicability screen, not a predictive benchmark. It yields two targets with noncommensurate adverse-event definitions/exposure/follow-up, no independently adjudicated target endpoint, no independent same-unit target groups, unresolved treatment attribution, and no clinical reviewer. A valid comparison is unavailable with this evidence pool; this does not show that all such benchmarks are impossible. These records cannot train or validate a target-level predictor. No AUC or clinical label update was made. **Fails as primary extension; retain as evidence normalization with review pending.** See `evidence/clinical_observations.tsv`, `clinical_sources.tsv`, `clinical_overlaps.tsv`, and `clinical_gap_delta.tsv`.
