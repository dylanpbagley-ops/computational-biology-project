## Local GSE193531 diagnostic

The locked matrix checks passed for all retained donors: 9 NBM donors/9,329 retained-eligible cells and 8 MM donors/10,759 neoplastic cells; 31 MM-stage Normal-labeled cells were excluded. The common depth was 5,189 UMIs/cell; 100 hypergeometric thinning draws used seed `20261003`, with the same retained cells compared before and after. Every source `n_counts` total matched the raw-count matrix. Median retained fraction was 86.3% in NBM and 73.5% in MM; MM-1 and MM-8 retained only 20.7% and 26.2%.

Within-stage donor-level associations between median depth and the three marker summaries varied by marker and summary. Examples include NBM normalized tail fraction Spearman rho of -0.55 (TNFRSF17) and +0.78 (GPRC5D), and MM normalized detection fraction rho of +0.60 (TNFRSF17) and +0.79 (SLAMF7). These use 9 or 8 donor points, describe association only, and do not identify a causal technical effect. The local GSE193531 cell file does not provide age, sex, preparation, source batch, or immunoglobulin-composition covariates, so those explanations cannot be separated from biology in this matrix.

## External GSE305971 join and source-label limits

The annotation workbook selected 65,508 cells across the locked first-biopsy/BM/MARS code frame; 62,811 (95.9%) joined exactly to a count-table cell ID and `Amp.Batch.ID`. The remaining 2,697 IDs were retained as unmatched, not assigned zero expression. There were 2 Healthy/Normal_PC unmatched, 2,653 MM/Malignant unmatched, and 42 MM/Normal_PC prior-art-comparator unmatched. The unmatched MM cells are concentrated in `cart_p20`, `028-0604-002`, `028-0608-008`, `028-0608-003`, and `tlv50`. A bounded namespace check found `W...` count-table IDs but `WMC...` workbook IDs in one affected partition; no source-verified alias/crosswalk was supplied, so no prefix/position repair was performed.

The workbook includes `Amp.Batch.ID` and `Seq.Batch.ID`, and count joins were keyed to exact `Amp.Batch.ID`; these fields allow partition-aware checks but do not by themselves identify the cause of expression variation or separate processing effects from biology. The workbook lacks complete age, sex, preparation, and cell-composition covariates needed for that separation. `Hospital.Code` is used conservatively to prevent same-code leakage, but source documents do not establish it as a globally unique person identifier. A second sample/biopsy can represent a paired specimen or assay partition; those were not automatically treated as contradictions. The 668 source-wide Healthy/BM/Malignant records (659 joined in the selected manifest) have unresolved biology/label semantics. They are excluded from the primary source-defined Healthy+Normal_PC reference and reported separately in the locked uncertain-label sensitivity. Their annotation is not evidence that they are true normal cells or true malignant cells.

Therefore all external estimates are conditional on the selected source labels, exact matched cell subset, putative code grouping, and MARS integer-molecule normalization. Worst-case missingness bounds are arithmetic endpoints over the workbook-eligible but unjoined IDs, not confidence intervals or imputed values. No technical, biological, patient-overlap, tissue-wide, or clinical cause is established by these files.
