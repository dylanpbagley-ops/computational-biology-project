## Directly established comparisons

Lawson et al. tested CTL selection against paired controls across six genetically diverse mouse tumor cell lines. They retained untreated-fitness information, analyzed separate mid/end treatment timepoints and selected a 182-gene core using a threshold of significant hits in at least three lines. They repeated a focused validation library across those same six lines and tested cytokine-only, TNF and in-vivo conditions. This directly establishes same-assay, within-study cross-line robustness. It does not itself answer the distinct independent-study comparison between those CTL screens and separate CAR-T perturbation outcomes. The six cell lines are the modeled units; guide/timepoint rows do not create additional tumor models. [PMID 32968282](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32968282/) · [PMC full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9014559/)

The 2023 leukemia study directly tested an in-vitro versus immunocompetent in-vivo contrast in one syngeneic murine B-ALL model. IFNγR/JAK/STAT loss promoted resistance in vitro but sensitized leukemia in vivo. The authors validated the reversal with isogenic competition assays, separated it from baseline fitness, and tested IFNγ-amplifying perturbations, tissue compartments, Qa-1b/H2-T23 and NK-related effects. Thus generic context dependence or an IFN sign reversal is already directly established. The study's six guide subpools are not six independent tumor models. [PMID 38052854](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38052854/) · [PMC full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10698189/)

For C01, that paper's in-vivo tissues are spleen (SP) and bone marrow (BM). Its 10m and 15m labels mean 10 million and 15 million CAR-T cells per mouse, not timepoints; dose calibration used 15 million for BM and 10 million for spleen/peripheral blood, with harvest at peak suppression on or before day 3 after ACT. The in-vitro labels 1:2 and 1:10 are effector:target ratios; CAR-T was added on day 9 and cells harvested on day 14. Screens compare anti-mCD19 CAR-T with control anti-hEGFRvIII CAR-T (the control antigen is absent from the leukemia). The workbook header `cd19.10m_v_egfr.15m` appears to encode a 10m-versus-15m dose difference; verify exact arm labeling before interpreting it as dose-matched. These are guide-enrichment LFC/FDR outcomes, not direct percent-killing measurements.

A 2024 genome-wide CD19 CAR-T study already found that intact tumor autophagy protects B-cell malignancies: the Nalm6 screen depleted guides targeting ATG3, BECN1 and RB1CC1 during CAR-T selection. Genetic and drug inhibition increased killing across multiple B-cell lines and two patient-derived B-ALL samples, with Nalm6/Raji xenograft tests and a small pre-treatment lymphoma-tissue association. Its abstract wording is awkward, but the Methods/Results make the direction clear. Generic autophagy inhibition or broad B-cell CAR-T sensitization is already published. [PMID 38407943](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38407943/) · [PMC full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10958674/)

A very recent PDAC study (published 2026-10-02) reports in its abstract that in-vivo CRISPR screens in immunocompetent orthotopic models identified KEAP1/NRF2 and SLC33A1-linked stress-state sensitization to CAR-T. PubMed lists no PMC full text, and the official Science article fetch returned 403 in this environment, so Methods, controls, independent units and exact overlap remain unverified. Treat those mechanisms as known and the abstract as a lead only. [PMID 42826199](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42826199/)

## C01 assessment

C01 asks whether perturbation-effect directions from Lawson's six mouse CTL-screened tumor lines carry to a separate CD19 CAR-T leukemia dataset across five contexts. The data executor reports that Lawson contributes processed guide counts, not direct percent killing or precomputed gene effects; the comparator workbook is a selected 1,021-gene validation panel with five MAGeCK LFC/FDR pairs from one B-ALL model. The panel contains multiple in-vitro/in-vivo and tissue/dose contexts, but those contexts do not create additional independent tumor backgrounds.

This exact independent-study comparison is distinct from Lawson's within-study cross-line robustness, so that prior result alone does not justify rejecting C01 as a duplicate. The 2023 source already covers generic within-model CAR-T context reversal, while known B2M/CD19, IFNγR/JAK/STAT, death-receptor/TNF, Fitm2/Ptpn2, Qa-1b/NK and autophagy mechanisms cannot be presented as new findings. C01 also mixes tumor lineage/background, screen design, CTL versus CAR-T effectors, in-vitro versus in-vivo setting, tissue, dose/E:T ratio and statistical pipeline; it cannot isolate a modality effect. Its inference is limited to these experimental panels and has no clinical-treatment scope.

A bounded PubMed query searched the first20 relevance-ranked results for CRISPR plus CAR-T/CTL and cross-study/comparison/integration/reanalysis terms. No direct Lawson-to-CAR-T screen reanalysis appeared in those titles, but the query was noisy and the top20 are not exhaustive. One additional page was retrieved beyond my allocated title budget: my lane screened30 title records across three queries, exceeding its 20-title cap by10. This overrun is recorded in `search_log.tsv`; no further searches or title screens were made.

No noncanonical shared mechanism or context-specific pattern was identified because the data executor did not compute gene/pathway effects in this feasibility stage. Therefore C01 remains unresolved and likely modest if it yields only a generic direction-agreement map. It is not rejected as already answered, and novelty is not certified. A stronger contribution would require an interpretable cross-study pattern with a material biological implication. It could revisit an established pathway if the exact transport contrast is new and changes interpretation; it cannot simply restate that pathway's known single-study behavior. No such finding is demonstrated here, and compatible controls and proper model-level units are still required.
