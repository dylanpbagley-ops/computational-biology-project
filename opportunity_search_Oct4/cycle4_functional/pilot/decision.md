# Discriminating pilot result

The fixed descriptive pilot returned five primary flags (Med16, Nprl3, Cbfa2t3, Etv5, Irf1) and zero symmetric flags among 1,021 selected validation-panel genes; 847 had exact-symbol matches in the Lawson count matrix. Each primary flag had sign-stable sensitizing direction in at least three of six latest CTL contexts and positive Ramos LFC with reported FDR<=0.05 in both in-vitro conditions. The observed values and context lists are in `transport_flags.tsv`; raw-count-derived guide effects and exclusions are in `gene_effects.tsv` and `guide_eligibility.tsv`.

The result is an exploratory cross-study pattern, not direct killing, a reproduced drugZ/MAGeCK analysis, causal evidence, independent prospective validation, a novel mechanism, or a clinical result. Med16 is already reported as an in-vivo EMT6 sensitizer in Lawson; Irf1 is an IFN-pathway anchor. Early/late stability is incomplete (Med16 3/4, Nprl3 2/3, Cbfa2t3 0/3, Etv5 2/3, Irf1 3/4 among each gene's latest-sensitizing contexts). Parent independently checked 720 raw-count pair/summary values and all 1,021 flag decisions (maximum numerical discrepancy 3.55e-15). Exact scientific limits and the decision gate are recorded in the stage `decision.md`.
