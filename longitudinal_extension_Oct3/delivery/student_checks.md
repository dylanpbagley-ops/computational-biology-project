# Student explanation checks

Before using these results in any school discussion, Dylan should be able to explain in their own words:

- Which primary source supports the PM01, PM07, and PM10 RNA sample pairs, and why PM01's P1426 DNA sample cannot replace P1393 RNA.
- Why the analysis counts CD138-selected barcodes rather than claiming that every barcode is a confirmed malignant cell.
- What the four joint detection categories show, how the directed conditional denominator is defined, and why joint counts and per-marker marginals must be shown together.
- Why the 44,981 barcodes remain subsamples of three participants and do not support a population treatment effect.
- Why the post-venetoclax label is a specimen-time contrast and does not prove causation, lineage tracking, protein loss, or antigen escape.
- What depth difference was observed for PM10, which explanations remain unresolved, what joint hypergeometric thinning preserves, and why the replicate range is not a patient confidence interval.
- What cannot be resolved from the sample-level v2 versus series-level v3 metadata disagreement.
- Why the project stops at a feasibility/pilot result rather than claiming a substantial extension or validated predictor.

Dylan authors any submitted paper, abstract, poster, or research plan; this file is a comprehension checklist, not text to submit.
