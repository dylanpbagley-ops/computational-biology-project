# Factual research-contribution assessment

This stage resolved one sample-identity gap and executed a locked pilot on three verified pre/post-venetoclax RNA participant pairs. It establishes that the six public GSE199359 filtered H5 matrices are accessible and structurally analyzable for the fixed TNFRSF17, GPRC5D, and SLAMF7 panel, and provides specimen-level joint transcript-detection tables plus a joint-depth sensitivity analysis.

The pilot does not establish malignant-cell identity per barcode, surface antigen availability, functional escape, cell-lineage continuity, a causal venetoclax effect, a prediction method, generalization, or a substantial scientific extension. Participant-level changes were heterogeneous, and PM10's post specimen had substantially lower RNA depth. The exact three-pair contrast remains narrowly distinct from the accessible main-text results of the closest sources, but relevant supplements were not fully checked.

The defensible contribution of this stage is a transparent, reproducible feasibility result and a negative decision on full execution for this candidate. Do not state that the substantial-extension objective was achieved. Dylan writes all submitted prose; this document records factual assistance only.
