# G1 — narrow research-gap decision

## Closest primary evidence

- Yao et al., Cancer Research 2023 (DOI 10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-22-1769; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10102848/) analyzed 53 marrow samples from 41 people. Figure 4 already examines co-expression and mutual exclusion of candidate surface targets in single cells and includes flow-cytometry checks. Its target set includes TNFRSF17, GPRC5D, and SLAMF7. Figure 6 compares longitudinal samples from six people and describes target expression changes across disease progression, including TNFRSF17 and SLAMF7. Generic same-cell complementarity and longitudinal target expression are therefore established.
- Leblay et al., Blood 2024 (DOI 10.1182/blood.2023020276; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10797556/) profiles pre/post-venetoclax single-cell RNA/ATAC in t(11;14) MM. Its main reported contrast is venetoclax-associated B-cell-like to plasma-cell-like subcluster/state change and related resistance biology; the accessible main text does not report joint detection distributions for this fixed three-target panel.
- Kwon et al., Nature Biotechnology 2023 (DOI 10.1038/s41587-023-01686-y) establishes computational combinatorial-target selection and Boolean coverage/specificity logic. No new target-selection algorithm or prediction superiority is proposed.
- Lee et al. 2023 and 2026 (DOIs 10.1038/s41591-023-02491-5 and 10.1038/s41591-025-04175-8) already establish antigen-targeted escape mechanisms. This pilot does not claim antigen escape or functional loss.

## Exact retained contrast

In the three verified PM01/PM07/PM10 RNA pairs, describe whether the joint transcript-detection distributions for the fixed TNFRSF17/GPRC5D/SLAMF7 panel differ between CD138-selected pre- and post-venetoclax specimens. This is treatment-context-specific descriptive work; it is not prediction, a method comparison, protein co-expression, lineage tracing, or a causal treatment effect.

## Decision

**G1: narrow conditional pass for descriptive feasibility only.** The exact fixed-panel joint distribution across this pre/post-venetoclax t(11;14) cohort is not reported in the accessible main text of the closest papers. Yao et al. already address the broad underlying biology, and the relevant supplements were not accessible in this pass, so the incremental novelty is narrow and remains conditional. The prediction/selection-method branch fails G1 and is closed. A three-person pilot cannot by itself establish substantial originality or a competitive-strength extension.
