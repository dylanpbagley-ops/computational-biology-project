# Pilot figure captions

**Figure 1 — Paired joint-category change from deposited counts.** Each point is a participant-specific post-minus-pre difference in the fraction of all filtered-matrix barcodes in one of the four same-cell transcript-detection categories. Detection is raw deposited Gene Expression UMI count >0. Panels use the three fixed pairs and target order named before matrix retrieval. Points are descriptive participant-paired values; there are no cell-level p-values or uncertainty intervals. These are CD138-selected RNA barcodes, not per-cell malignant labels. Values are in `figure_joint_category_deltas.tsv`.

**Figure 2 — Raw versus joint depth-sensitivity change in double-undetected fraction.** For each participant and fixed target pair, circles show the post-minus-pre fraction of barcodes with neither target detected from deposited counts; squares show the mean of 100 deterministic multivariate-hypergeometric thinning replicates at D=1,318. Each cell draw jointly samples the three target counts and all other RNA counts without replacement. The mean is a sensitivity summary, not an uncertainty interval or participant-level estimate. Values are in `figure_depth_sensitivity_values.tsv`; the underlying replicate results are in `depth_sensitivity_paired_deltas.tsv`.
