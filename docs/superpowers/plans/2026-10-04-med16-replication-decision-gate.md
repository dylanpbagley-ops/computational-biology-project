# MED16: one computational replication and project-selection gate

## Purpose and present decision

MED16 is a conditional lead, not the selected substantive extension. The completed pilot found tumor-cell MED16 perturbation sensitizing in three Lawson mouse CTL contexts at both sampled stages, with resistance-direction enrichment in both Ramos mouse B-ALL CAR-T dish conditions. Those observations came from published experimental data. The parent independently verified their arithmetic. Baseline growth, unequal harvest days, differing tumor backgrounds/antigens/effectors and exploratory scoring prevent a causal explanation.

The next question is precise: **does an independent tumor-side immune-killing dataset reproduce the MED16 resistance-direction effect under CAR-T, with enough controls to distinguish ordinary growth or antigen loss?** Do not start a broad opportunity search. Do not use macrophage MHC-II/CD4 results as evidence of tumor MHC-I/CD8 or CD19 mechanisms. A replicated descriptive contrast alone may still be too modest; project selection requires a useful, distinct biological question that the accessible data can answer.
